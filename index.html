About Me

Who I Am as a Developer

I have played video games all my life. They have always been a way to escape the troubles of daily life, to have fun with friends, or simply decompress. After nearly a decade as a paramedic, I have needed to decompress a lot. As time went on I started to notice games coming out less polished and a clear focus on politicization or profit over experience. This plus my love for games led me to decide to join the game development industry. I would like to bring a renaissance to gaming. I want to make game that a younger version of my friends and I could have gotten lost in.

C++ Console Application

BattleLine

BattleLine is a text-based battle simulation game. The game includes 2 different modes for turn-based combat. Damage and healing are randomized based on a particular unit's damage and/or healing ranges.

Key Features

  • Attacking and defending unit selection.
  • Randomized damage and healing based on unit-specific stats.
  • Inputs are validated through a try-catch methodology featuring a loop and prompt to assist user correction of response.

What I Learned

I had difficulty storing and displaying my units without slicing, but using smart pointers and dynamic casts let me store each army in its own vector and avoid the slicing I had experienced.
